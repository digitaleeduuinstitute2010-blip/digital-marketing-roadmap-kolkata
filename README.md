Digital Marketing Roadmap for Beginners in Kolkata (2026)

A free, practical guide for students, freshers and working professionals who want to start a career in digital marketing. It covers the skills to learn, the tools to practise with, and how to choose a course.

Who This Guide Is For
Students and graduates looking for a first career path
Job seekers switching into digital marketing
Freelancers and business owners who want to promote their own business online

No technical background is needed.

Learning Roadmap

Stage 1: Foundations

[ ] Understand how digital marketing works (SEO, ads, social, email, content)
[ ] Learn how websites and search engines work
[ ] Set up a free blog or WordPress site to practise on

Stage 2: Core Skills

[ ] SEO: keyword research, on-page, off-page, technical and local SEO
[ ] Google Ads: search and display campaigns, conversion tracking
[ ] Social media marketing: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn
[ ] Content marketing: blog planning, copywriting, content strategy
[ ] Email marketing: campaigns, automation, lead nurturing

Stage 3: Measurement

[ ] Google Analytics (GA4)
[ ] Google Search Console
[ ] Basic performance reporting for clients

Stage 4: AI-Assisted Marketing

[ ] Draft SEO-friendly blog outlines and meta titles with ChatGPT or Gemini
[ ] Create ad copy variations for Google Ads
[ ] Generate social media creatives with Canva AI
[ ] Understand email automation with HubSpot or Mailchimp

Stage 5: Build Proof

[ ] Run a small live project (your own site or a local business)
[ ] Create an SEO report and an ad performance summary
[ ] Build a simple portfolio and a LinkedIn profile

How to Choose a Digital Marketing Course in Kolkata

Ask these questions before you enrol:

Is there a free demo class?
What is the batch size? Smaller batches mean more personal feedback.
Will I work on live projects, not just demo assignments?
Are the trainers currently working on real campaigns?
Does the syllabus include AI tools and GA4?
How many students from the last batch were placed, in what roles and how quickly?
What are the total fees and EMI options?

Be careful with "100% placement" claims. Ask for specific numbers instead.

Online vs Offline Learning
Offline: better for direct interaction and peer learning, useful if you live near Dum Dum, Salt Lake, New Town, Garia or Howrah.
Online: better for working professionals or students outside Kolkata, as long as live sessions and mentor feedback are included.
